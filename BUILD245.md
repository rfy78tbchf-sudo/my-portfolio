The read-only realized-sales RPC preserves every original amount, status and count. A new optional average_cost_estimate attaches actual net sale proceeds minus sold quantity times the latest positive broker average observed before the sale date, within seven days, on the same account/security/currency. Pending executions use collected sale proceeds and explicitly mark selling costs unfinalized. Exact broker asset IDs in the account ledger resolve duplicate symbol registry entries; conflicting identities remain uncalculated. Known intervening transfers/splits block an estimate. Legacy backfills, same-day/post-sale snapshots and stale/zero averages are rejected. Foreign reference KRW uses an available sale-day-or-prior seven-day FX rate and stays missing when FX is unavailable.

These are reference estimates, not reconstructed moving-average allocations: later purchases can change actual cost, and multiplying foreign profit by a reference FX rate does not include acquisition-to-sale currency gains. The existing realized display opts in, labels both total and rows as estimated, and exposes average basis date/cost status in its existing disclosure. Reconciled broker amounts and complete closed-cycle results take priority. Original period P&L, dividends/cash flows, currency grouping and verified decomposition do not adopt these estimates.
